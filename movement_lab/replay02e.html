The unreliable sense of upright.

What happens when Microduck's sense of upright is a little wrong? Each panel replays a saved MuJoCo/BAM trial under the same external condition. Below, compare the trunk's true pitch with the pitch its controller perceived.

Filled sole = geometric foot contactOrange dot = whole-body centre of massSolid trace = true trunk pitch · dashed = pitch the controller perceived

Every frame is a saved simulation state from the native MuJoCo/BAM model with the hash-matching compiled binary, recorded at 200 Hz and shown at 50 Hz. The drawing joins measured body frames and projects geometry-derived sole outlines; it is a schematic, not a mesh render, and runs no browser physics. Perceived pitch is what the controller received, including misalignment, noise and any calibration front-end. Outcome labels apply the unchanged 02D whole-trial audit. A late replant cannot erase a fall. Comparisons were declared before rendering and include failures. No hardware, stepping or locomotion claim.
